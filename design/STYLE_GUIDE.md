# Style Guide

## Colors
- **--cream**: `#FBF5E6` (warm butter background)
- **--ink**: `#2C1810` (warm dark brown-black for text and outlines)
- **--coral**: `#FF6B47` (sunny coral/orange accent)
- **--sky**: `#47B3E8` (sky blue accent)
- **--sun**: `#FFD23F` (sunshine yellow accent)
- **--leaf**: `#5EC269` (grass/leaf green accent)
- **--cream-dark**: `#F0E8D0` (slightly darker cream for subtle surfaces)
- **--ink-light**: `#6B4C3B` (lighter warm brown for secondary text)

## Typography
- **Display/Headline**: "Fredoka", rounded, bouncy.
- **Body/UI**: "Nunito", rounded terminals, warm, readable.
- **Scale**: defined in CSS `--text-xs` through `--text-7xl`.

## Illustration Recipe
- **Stroke width**: 2.5–3px
- **Colors**: Fill from palette, stroke is always `--ink`
- **Corners**: Rounded corners (r=4 on SVG shapes)
- **Style**: No gradients, slight hand-drawn feel, flat vector cartoon.

## Mascot: Pip the Reading Owl
- Perfectly round cream body with warm brown outline (3px stroke, #2C1810)
- Two big circular eyes with warm brown irises and small highlight dots
- Small pointed ears/tufts on top
- Tiny orange beak triangle
- Two small wings folded at sides
- Tiny open book held in front (fanned pages)

## Radius
- 24px for large cards, fully rounded (9999px) for bubbles and pills.

## Shadows
- Cartoon offset shadow: `4px 4px 0px --ink` at a low opacity or full opacity depending on the element.
